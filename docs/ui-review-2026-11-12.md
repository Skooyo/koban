# UI review, 2026-11-12

Five read-only reviews of the app against the NN/G 10 usability heuristics, the frontend-design skill and the phone-relevant rules of design-taste-frontend, with the approved group screen (layout D) as the consistency reference. Nothing is changed yet; the owner picks what to fix.

## Do now (highest user impact)

1. **Typed data is lost with no warning.** Payment screen X and Back (`PaymentScreen.kt:173`, no `BackHandler`), the edit sheet swipe-down (`CheckPaymentCard.kt:274-293`), and split bill Back on Items while editing a saved bill (`SplitEditorViewModel.kt:439-442`). Fix: "Discard changes?" dialog when the form changed. (#3, #5)
2. **Actions with no confirm or undo.** "Mark paid" on a "You owe" row (`PersonScreen.kt:555`), category delete (`SettingsScreen.kt:182-203`), "Remove online access" (`SettingsScreen.kt:259`). Fix: snackbar with Undo, or a confirm dialog for online access. (#3, #5)
3. **One app-wide system.** No `Shapes` in the theme: 21 `RoundedCornerShape` uses with 11 radius values. About 37 spacing values off the 8 dp grid; screen edges 20 dp (approved: 16 dp), 12 dp between blocks (approved: 24 dp). 5 or more button styles on People. Fix: `Shapes(small 8, medium 12, large 16, extraLarge 24)`, 16 dp edges, 24 dp between blocks, one filled button per screen. (#4, #8)
4. **Same idea, different words.** Expense/Income vs Spent/Received; Merchant vs Shop; "Not a payment" vs "Skipped"; "You pay RM 5" vs "You owe". Fix: one word per idea (glossary). (#2, #4)
5. **Settings has no order.** Developer tools first, tracking status split across a banner, a red sentence and grey text. Fix: sections Tracking, Categories, Receipts, Appearance, then Troubleshooting last; status rows with an icon and words. (#1, #4, #8)

## Later

- Split bill: "Step 2 of 5" under the title; Fix sheet re-opens by itself and hides a failed "Read again" (`SplitEditorScreen.kt:235-238, 345-352`); name the field that blocks Next; mismatch text says over or under ("Add RM -0.05" today); Assign screen all red before any tap; Summary rows "Ali owes you"; Reading step has no Cancel.
- Home and Activity: gold has 4 meanings (Add, waiting banner, selected week, Entertainment); 3 banner styles; selected week chip hard to see; "＋ Add" → "Add payment"; "No payments recorded yet" → "No payments today".
- Payments: Undo pill is text, not a button, and 4 s is short for TalkBack; the "new category applies to past payments" warning sits far from Category; save errors are not caught (`PaymentViewModel.kt:127-139`); fields as separate cards (use one surface with dividers, as the check card does).
- People: direction by color only; Settle up dialog states counts, not money; Delete person does not mention open balances; "Mark paid" TalkBack label lacks the item name.
- Settings and navigation: "Payment apps" row has no chevron and hides "none chosen"; invite field label is "XXXX-XXXX" (label "Invite code", placeholder the format); dark-mode row only the Switch is tappable; tab icons read twice by TalkBack; PaymentApps Back drops changes; help paragraphs become an (i) button.
- Theme: light-theme contrast below 4.5:1 for `received` and `goldText`; `inverseSurface` unset (purple-grey switch); debug screen has light pastel cards in dark theme; possible double insets on the split bill and debug screens (check on a device).

## Keep

Lists with dividers on Home, Activity, People and the check card; 48 dp targets almost everywhere; TalkBack work on the amount, donut and swipe actions; delete dialogs with plain consequences; the QR in its gold frame; `ListenerStatusBanner` as the model for status rows; one primary action per split-bill step.
