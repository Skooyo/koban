Split a bill with less typing

Today each item is 3 typed fields, you work out qty × price and the percentages yourself, and you walk the list a second time to say who had what.

The new flow picks people first, fills items from a scan or from recent items at the shop, multiplies on a keypad, and splits whatever is left evenly in one tap.

Step 2 of 6, Items, in the app today.

  1. 3 fields per item. Item, Qty and Line price, typed for all 8 lines, with a switch between text and number keyboards each time.
  2. You do the math. Line price is qty × unit price (2 × 12.50). Service charge and SST are RM fields, so you work out 10% and 6%. Rounding needs a typed minus sign.
  3. Assign in a second pass. People come after items, then "Who had what" walks the whole list again.
